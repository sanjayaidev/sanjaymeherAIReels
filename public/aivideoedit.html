FAQs

What exactly is "AI reel editing"?

I use AI tools to generate motion, glow, holographic effects, and scene transitions — which gives your reel a premium 3D-style look without a studio shoot. It's not a filter; it's a creative workflow.

How long is each reel?

Typically 30 to 40 seconds, optimised for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok.

What do I need to provide?

Just the raw footage (or product photos), any branding assets (logo, colors), and a brief on the vibe you want. I'll handle the rest.

How many revisions are included?

2 revisions per single reel, and up to 3 for the package. Custom projects get a flexible revision policy.

Can you match my brand's existing style?

Yes — I can adapt the AI effects and motion to fit your brand's visual identity. Just share references.

Do you offer a trial?

You can order a single reel to test the style and quality before committing to a package.
